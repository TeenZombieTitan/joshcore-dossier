Terms of use

The rules, such as they are.

This is a hobby project for one Discord server, not a product. These terms are short because there isn't much to say — but using the site means you're fine with what's below.

What this is

Blackbox is a Discord bot and a website that publishes what it recorded. It's free, it's run by one person, and it can stop working, change, or disappear at any time without notice.

Using the site

Don't:

  • Scrape it in bulk, hammer it with automated requests, or try to break it.
  • Use anything here to harass, dox or threaten anyone on it.
  • Repost people's names, screenshots or stats somewhere they'd obviously object to.
  • Try to get at data belonging to someone who has sealed their file.

The information here is about real people who didn't volunteer for it. Treat it the way you'd want yours treated.

Your content

Screenshots and rumours you post in Discord stay yours. Posting them in the bound channels means you're happy for them to appear on this site, and you can take them down any time with ~pull or the ❌ reaction.

Don't post anything you don't have the right to post, anything illegal, or anything you wouldn't want on a public web page — because that is exactly where it goes.

The games

Supply Drop and Breach Point run entirely in your browser and connect directly to your opponent's. No account, no server in the middle, nothing stored. Room codes are temporary and anyone who has the code can join, so only share them with people you want in your game.

Moderation

Server staff can remove anything from the site at their discretion — screenshots, rumours, or an entire file. There's no appeals process; it's a Discord server, ask them.

No guarantees

Everything here is provided as-is. The numbers are what the bot happened to record and may be wrong, incomplete, or lost entirely if something breaks. Nothing on this site should be relied on for anything that matters, and no liability is accepted for any of it.

Privacy

What's collected and how to remove it is covered properly on the privacy page. That one's worth actually reading.

Questions

Ask in the Discord server. That's the only support channel there is.

Written in plain English by the person who runs this, not by a lawyer. It's a statement of how this thing is intended to work, not a contract drafted by anyone qualified.
